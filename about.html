Our story & mission

Little people.
Big possibilities.

Arise & Shine Early Learning Centre is a place for young children and families in Rustenburg. Our name says what we believe: a bright future starts with the care, encouragement and learning children experience today.

Our mission is to offer a warm, encouraging start where children can explore, grow in confidence and feel proud of each new step.

We want little learners to feel welcome as they explore the world around them, discover what they love and take pride in the things they can do.

Learning through
