Documents to ask about

Requirements can vary. When enquiring, ask the centre which documents are needed. Families are often asked to prepare:

  • Child’s birth certificate or identification document
  • Parent or guardian identification
  • Immunisation or clinic card
  • Emergency contact and authorised collection details

This is a preparation guide, not the centre’s confirmed registration checklist. Please verify requirements directly.

R

Fees & enrolment terms

Contact the centre for the current fee schedule and written enrolment terms. Before registering, ask about:

  • Registration or once-off charges
  • Monthly fees, due dates and payment methods
  • What is included in the fees
  • Notice periods, absences and collection arrangements
  • Transport availability and any related charges

Fees, payment rules and enrolment terms have not been provided here; confirm them with the centre before committing.
